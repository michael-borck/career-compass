Assess

See what you have versus what you need, get a step-by-step learning path to job-ready, and map your skills into professional frameworks.

Build what you need

Draft a cover letter, write a 30–60 second elevator pitch, get structured feedback on your resume, and rehearse interview questions.

Bring your own AI

Fully local, by default

Point Career Compass at Ollama and everything — resume, analysis, conversation — stays on your machine. The app even walks you through setting it up.

Or any major provider

Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Your key, your choice, one settings tab each.

Keys kept safe

API keys are encrypted with your operating system's native secure storage — Keychain on macOS, Credential Manager on Windows, libsecret on Linux.

Privacy-first, by design

Career decisions are personal. Career Compass is built so your resume, your doubts, and your five-year plans are not on someone else's server.

  • No account, no telemetry. Install it and start exploring.
  • Files processed locally. PDF and Word resumes are parsed on your device.
  • Sessions stay on disk. Your results and chats live in local storage, exportable as a single Career Pack document.
  • Open source. MIT-licensed, auditable, yours.

For students & graduates

Turn a thin resume and a lot of questions into concrete directions: which paths fit, what the first job pays, how long the learning path takes, and what to say in the interview.

For career changers

Your experience counts for more than you think. Map your existing skills onto new fields, see the genuine gaps, and rehearse the story that connects where you've been to where you're going.
